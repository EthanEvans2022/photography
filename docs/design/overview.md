# Design Overview

## Brand & Identity

<!-- What's the overall feel of the site? Reference sites, mood boards, or adjectives. -->
This is going to be a site that functions as my own personal photo and video interface. The goal is to emulate that of the big Cloud storage sites like iCloud photos, Google Photos, and Microsoft OneDrive in terms of look and function. So I want it to be clean and minimal.
